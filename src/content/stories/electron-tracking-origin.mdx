## The problem

This technology originated from a real client requirement for desktop tracking — and
the required functionality was not fully available in existing solutions.

## What existed

Existing solutions handled the keyboard and mouse functionality.

## What was missing

None of them provided the required application tracking and web tracking functionality.

## What Sandbee engineered

Sandbee built the missing components manually: native, cross-operating-system pieces
covering application tracking and web tracking — completing a desktop tracking
technology that spans keyboard, mouse, application and web activity.

## What became reusable

The resulting technology is intended to become a reusable package/service for other
developers who need similar functionality. That is the broader Sandbee pattern: a real
client problem becomes a technical solution; the solution becomes reusable internal
technology; the technology becomes a package, service or product for multiple customers.

Not every client project should automatically become a product. Productization should
make sense: repeatable value, broader demand, reusable architecture, a clear customer
benefit and viable commercial potential.
